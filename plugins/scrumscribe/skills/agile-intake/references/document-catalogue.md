# Document catalogue

Offer this list during setup. The team keeps only what it picks in `charter.yaml` under `docs_wanted`. The id in the first column is the value to use there.

| id | Document | What it answers | When it applies | Usual owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| product-goal | Product Goal | Where is the product heading? | Always (Scrum commitment) | PO |
| sprint-goal | Sprint Goal | What does this sprint deliver? | Every sprint (Scrum commitment) | Scrum team |
| dod | Definition of Done | When is work truly finished? | Always (Scrum commitment) | Scrum Team (or an organisation standard) |
| dor | Definition of Ready | When can a story enter a sprint? | Once per team | PO + team |
| prd | PRD or pitch | What problem, for whom, within what appetite? | Every epic | PO |
| story-map | Story map | How do the stories form a user journey? | New product or large epic | PO + team |
| fr-nfr-spec | FR / NFR specification | What must it do, and how well? | Every epic | PO, reviewed by tech lead |
| adr | Architecture decision record | Why did we choose this design? | Hard-to-reverse technical choices | Tech lead |
| test-plan | Test plan or strategy | How will we prove it works? | Integration or high-risk work | QA lead |
| eval-plan | Evaluation plan | How good is the model, and what is the bar? | AI-driven features | ML lead |
| raci | Team charter and RACI | Who owns, decides and reviews what? | Once per team | Scrum Master |
| raid | Risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies log | What could block us, and who resolves it? | Work across teams | Scrum Master |
| privacy | Privacy assessment | Is personal data handled lawfully? | Any personal data | PO + privacy or legal |
| release | Release notes and runbook | What changed, and how is it operated? | Each release | Tech lead |
| retro | Retrospective notes | What do we change next sprint? | Every sprint | Scrum Master |

The Scrum Guide 2020 attaches one commitment to each artifact: the Product Goal to the Product Backlog, the Sprint Goal to the Sprint Backlog, and the Definition of Done to the Increment ([Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html)).
